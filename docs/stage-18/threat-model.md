# Stage 18 — Threat model

Method: STRIDE per trust boundary (security-architecture.md §1), with likelihood and impact on a 1–3 scale (L/M/H).
"Existing controls" lists only controls with code, configuration or a test behind them (control ids in
security-control-register.md). A risk is never marked mitigated because a design document exists. Updates the Stage 0
model (`docs/THREAT-MODEL.md`).

| ID | STRIDE | Component | Attack scenario | Impact | Likelihood | Existing controls | Missing controls | Residual | Required mitigation | Verification evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-01 | S | auth | **Account takeover** through a stolen password | H | M | Argon2id; MFA available for users, mandatory for staff; session rotation; new-device-independent revocation on password change (C-AUTH-01..06) | WebAuthn; breached-password check | M | phishing-resistant MFA for staff before production | `TestIdentityMFAEnrollLoginRecovery`, `TestIdentityPasswordResetAndChange` |
| T-02 | S | auth | **Credential stuffing** across many accounts | M | H | GCRA limits per IP and per email (never fail open), generic errors, dummy hash for unknown accounts; alerts `CREDENTIAL_STUFFING_SUSPECTED`, `ACCOUNT_TARGETED` | edge WAF / bot management | M | WAF at the edge (Stage 20) | `TestDistributedFallbackNeverFailsOpen`, `TestSecurityFindings` |
| T-03 | S,I | sessions | **Session hijacking** (cookie theft, fixation) | H | L | `__Host-`, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax; rotation at login; staff idle 15 min / 12 h; CSRF HMAC bound to the session | device binding | L | — | `TestCookiesAreHardened`, `TestIdentitySessionsRotationLogoutAndIDOR` |
| T-04 | E | auth, RBAC | **Privilege escalation** by a staff member (self-grant, step-up bypass) | H | M | role changes maker-checker (Go + DB); nobody decides their own access; step-up TOTP-only for staff — **password-change bypass fixed (F-S18-01)** | break-glass workflow (F-S18-04) | M | break-glass API with audit tagging | `TestIdentityRBACStaffMakerChecker`, `TestSecurityStaffPasswordChangeNeedsTOTPStepUp` |
| T-05 | I,E | all user-owned resources | **Tenant isolation failure** (IDOR across users/organisations) | H | M | server-side ownership checks; 404 for foreign ids; organisation membership checks; staff 404 for non-staff | RLS outside compliance | M | keep object-level tests in every module | `TestIdentityOrganisationIsolation`, `…SessionsRotationLogoutAndIDOR`, module ITs |
| T-06 | E,T | admin portal | **Administrative compromise** (stolen staff session) | H | L | MFA, short idle timeout, step-up for sensitive permissions, maker-checker on money and roles, no superuser, security alerts on break-glass and role changes | WebAuthn; admin network allow-list | M | admin access from a private network or SSO with device posture (Stage 20) | security-alert IT |
| T-07 | T | campaigns, discovery | **Campaign manipulation** (edits after approval, restricted campaign kept visible) | M | M | material edits re-reviewed; serve-time eligibility re-check; projection only proposes candidates | — | L | — | `TestDiscoveryEndToEnd`, campaign ITs |
| T-08 | T,R | payments | **Payment fraud** (forged success, browser-return confirmation) | H | M | only verified webhooks/polls/reconciliation change status (`payments.apply`); IN_FLIGHT before calls; UNKNOWN ≠ FAILED; no live execution | real provider controls | L | — | `TestPayments*` |
| T-09 | T | payouts | **Payout redirection** (destination changed before payout) | H | M | destination changes need fresh step-up and invalidate approvals; FINANCE maker-checker; destination hold; no real payout execution | — | L | — | `TestPayout*` |
| T-10 | T | ledger | **Ledger manipulation** (edit or delete posted journals) | H | L | only `fundzim_ledger` writes; journals POSTED immutable (triggers); corrections are maker-checker reversals; integrity checks L1–L15; runtime roles cannot UPDATE/DELETE | — | L | — | `TestLedger*`, `TestSecurityRuntimeRolesCannotRewriteFinancialHistory`, `fundzimctl verify ledger` |
| T-11 | T | reconciliation | **Reconciliation evidence tampering** (alter an imported statement) | H | L | SHA-256 first, immutable lines, revisions + CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE, evidence records, no DELETE grant | — | L | — | `TestReconciliation*` |
| T-12 | T,E | storage, recon | **Malicious statement upload** (CSV formula, markup, malware) | M | M | type sniffing per purpose, ClamAV before parsing, size limits, formula neutralisation on export, all-or-nothing parser | — | L | — | `TestReconciliationImportsAreImmutable…`, `sniff_test.go` |
| T-13 | S | webhooks | **Provider webhook forgery / replay** | H | M | HMAC with CURRENT/PREVIOUS secret, timestamp window, inbox dedupe, contradictory evidence escalated | — | L | — | `psp` tests, `TestPayments*` |
| T-14 | D,I | API | **API abuse** (scraping, unbounded queries) | M | H | per-IP and per-policy limits, bounded lists, cursor pagination, body limits, timeouts | edge rate limiting | M | WAF / CDN (Stage 20) | `ratelimit_idempotency_test.go`, ZAP API scan |
| T-15 | I | data stores | **Data exfiltration** (SQL injection, over-broad reads) | H | L | parameterised SQL only, archtest table ownership, field encryption for C3/C4, KYC pool isolation, no secrets in responses | DLP / egress monitoring | L | — | archtest, gosec |
| T-16 | I | config, logs, CI | **Secrets exposure** (repo, image, logs, bundle) | H | M | `check-secrets.sh` + gitleaks (history), no secrets in build args (test), log scrubbing, secretref-only provider credentials, `secretstore` keys | secret-manager audit device (production) | L | enable OpenBao audit device in production | `deploytest`, `TestErrorTextScrubbed`, CI secret scanning |
| T-17 | T | supply chain | **Supply-chain compromise** (dependency, action, base image) | H | M | SHA-pinned actions, digest-pinned images, govulncheck, npm audit, Trivy gate, SBOMs, checksum-verified tool downloads | artifact signing, dependency review bot | M | signing + provenance attestation (KI-S18-08) | CI images job, `TestWorkflowLeastPrivilege` |
| T-18 | T,I | backups | **Backup compromise** (read or alter a backup) | H | L | OpenPGP AES-256, key file outside the backup dir (refused otherwise), MAC manifest, restore refuses altered sets | off-host immutable storage, key escrow | M | object-lock storage and split key custody (production) | `failure-drills.sh` (corrupt, wrong key, tampered manifest, missing file) |
| T-19 | D,T | everything | **Ransomware** (encrypt or delete data and backups) | H | L | append-only financial history; backups encrypted under a separate key; restore drill measured | immutable off-host copies; host hardening | H | object-lock backups, least-privilege host access (Stage 20) | restore drill report |
| T-20 | D | API, workers | **Denial of service** | M | M | rate limits, timeouts, resource limits per container, queue isolation (payments, notifications, discovery, reconciliation, maintenance) | edge DDoS protection, autoscaling | M | CDN/WAF (Stage 20) | DR exercise, perf samples |
| T-21 | E,R | staff | **Insider abuse** (approve own payout, release own hold, read KYC without need) | H | M | maker-checker with linked identities (`app.actor_identities`), audit of every access, support roles get no KYC/bank/ledger access, access reviews | anomaly detection on staff behaviour | M | periodic access reviews (Stage 14 workflow) | `TestIdentityRBACStaffMakerChecker`, remediation ITs |

Residual H: T-19 (ransomware) until immutable off-host backups exist. Residual M items are production conditions listed
in production-readiness.md.
