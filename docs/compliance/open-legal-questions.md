# FundZim — Open Legal Questions (authoritative `LR` register)

**Stage 1 deliverable.** This file is now the **authoritative** Compliance Assumptions Register. It
supersedes the table in [../COMPLIANCE.md](../COMPLIANCE.md) §5, which keeps a pointer here. IDs are never
reused or renumbered. Research basis: [regulatory-landscape.md](regulatory-landscape.md) and
[regulatory-requirements-register.md](regulatory-requirements-register.md) (`REQ-xxx`). Last updated
**2026-10-08**.

> Every item is `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` unless it is marked as a business decision. No item has been
> reviewed by counsel. A resolved item needs a written determination, as set out in §6. Nothing in this
> register is a legal conclusion, and closing documentation items is not permission to operate.

## 1. Field definitions

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ID | `LR-xxx`, append-only |
| Area | Topic |
| Question / assumption | What counsel (or the business) must decide. Stage 1 research notes are appended in *italics*. |
| Why it matters | Consequence of getting it wrong |
| Blocked work / stage | What cannot proceed, or cannot go live, until it is resolved |
| Owner | **Counsel** = qualified Zimbabwean legal counsel; **Founders**; **Finance**; **DPO**; **Compliance officer**; **Eng** = technical lead; **Tax adviser**; **Payments lead** |
| Priority | **P0**: launch-blocking (no live pilot, or no enabling of the named feature, until resolved). **P1**: must be resolved before the related stage goes live. **P2**: important, not blocking. |
| Status | `OPEN`, `IN_REVIEW`, `RESOLVED` (with reference), `SUPERSEDED`, or `DUPLICATE` (of another ID) |
| Related | `REQ-xxx` rows, `PCR` provider questions, research checklist items (`RC-xx`) |

## 6. Maintenance rules

- **IDs.** New `LR` IDs are appended. IDs are never reused or renumbered. Duplicates are marked `DUPLICATE`
  (pointing to the surviving ID), not deleted.
- **Resolving an item** requires:
  - a written determination, or a reference to counsel's privileged memo stored outside the repository;
  - the date and the reviewer;
  - the affected `REQ` rows re-classified;
  - the resulting engineering change (an ADR if the change is architectural).
- **Code references.** Code that depends on an item references it near the policy configuration (for
  example, `// policy: LR-007`).
- **Stage reports.** Stage completion reports list every `LR` item touched, or still blocking, that stage.
- **Review cadence.** Monthly until the pilot, and immediately on any trigger in
  [regulatory-landscape.md](regulatory-landscape.md) §20.
